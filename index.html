Model pipeline

Species focus

Why edge-first?
Less bandwidth, lower latency, better privacy, and resilience when cellular connectivity fails.
On the live feed: browsers can't play a raw RTMP stream directly — it needs a media server (e.g. MediaMTX or nginx-rtmp) to convert it to HLS or WebRTC first. This console shows what that converted feed looks like in the operator view; wire in your media server's HLS URL to go fully live.
